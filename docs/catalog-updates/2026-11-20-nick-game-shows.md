## Sources and exclusions

`data/nick-game-show-selections.json` records reviewed public YouTube URLs through video identifiers, uploader names, source titles, durations and embedding flags. Selected sources were returned by current yt-dlp metadata discovery as embeddable, non-live recordings. Original-source thumbnails were inspected and cached as library card backgrounds. Later revivals, Pat Sajak's modern finale, reaction videos, retrospective documentaries, blooper excerpts and promo clips were excluded. Original host/series eras are retained in collection titles where multiple versions share a name. Missing canonical metadata remains unset rather than borrowing unrelated TVmaze results; original-era source references remain linked.

Nickelodeon's own historical game-show list corroborates the original program identities: https://ir.paramount.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nickelodeon-brings-back-popular-90s-game-series-figure-it-out . BUZZR corroborates the Classic Concentration rebus format: https://buzzrtv.com/videos . These references identify formats, not distribution permission.

Provider metadata and thumbnails do not establish full decoding, all-region availability, distribution permissions or continued playback. Videos remain with their providers. No AI artwork is generated. No new retrieval summaries/transcripts, companions, app binaries or channel identifiers are introduced by this content update.
