Counts identify playable source programs, including explicitly labeled bundles, rather than a completeness guarantee.

## Validation

Every imported source passed a small HTTP range check. One video sample from each of the 70 collections decoded with ffmpeg; additional frames clarified the mixed Remington Steele recording. These checks do not establish complete-file playback. Whole-disc files, menu clips, duplicate encodes, trailers, production extras and unrelated remakes are excluded by source rules. Unknown broadcast ordering remains season zero. Doctor Who serial parts are grouped. Red Dwarf is limited to seasons 1–8; Clone High uses the original season. Source provenance and selection rules are maintained in the discovery and source-specification JSON files.

The catalog import ceiling is 8 MB to accommodate UUID-based portable catalogs. The existing 256-show and 10,000-item limits remain. Channel AI accepts a bounded 20,000-character offered-show field in a 32 KB channel request; non-channel requests retain their 16 KB limit.
