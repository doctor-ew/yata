# Episode intent and tuning fixes

Release: 0.8.14. Date: 2026-11-19 UTC (November 18 local evening).

## Root causes and resulting behavior

The descriptive Street Hawk shortcut previously accepted any request containing Knight Rider and motorcycle. It ran before episode retrieval or show-level playback, so a named Knight Rider episode plot could create a Street Hawk channel locally. Chat transcripts are not retained by analytics; this finding comes from the routing implementation and regression reproduction, not a recovered four-hour-old conversation log.

The shortcut now requires an explicit show comparison, such as Knight Rider but with a motorcycle, and rejects episode/gang/plot wording. Watch and queue requests describing an episode enter episode lookup before show-channel commands. Named-series results cannot offer another series' evidence. A missing supported result leaves playback unchanged. The retrieval corpus still covers 86 Gargoyles/Street Hawk summaries; Knight Rider plot coverage was not added in this change.

The user's biker-gang description matches Good Day at White Rock. It is present in the catalog as Knight Rider S01 E04 Good Day At White Rock. Official Knight Rider clips identify it as season 1 episode 4; TVmaze uses episode 3. No catalog numbering or evidence reconciliation rule was silently changed.

- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8mYWbPOIKY
- https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/good-day-at-white-rock/umc.cmc.24ovnf0ta7d4ixkb5vfqffekg

Some Guide, Library and buddy paths dispose the player before the next source is started. The 0.8.13 stronger transition depended on the previous active show, so those paths received only faint grain and no new tuning audio. The new-source playback entry now consistently applies foreground tuning snow/sound without depending on the old player. Restoring a paused source does not trigger sound. Existing static/sound settings, mute, volume and accessibility suppression remain.

## Source additions

Added original live-action Swamp Thing (1990) to Sci-Fi with 72 checked entries and original Beauty and the Beast (1987) to Drama with 55. Their Archive byte-range endpoints and video content types passed. Uploader numbering and one MP4 per presentation are retained. Original-series artwork and metadata references are in data/library-art.json. No video is rehosted, and endpoint checks do not establish full decoding or future availability.

## Validation

293 Node tests and syntax checks passed. Browser reproduction verified that the biker-gang request enters retrieval, does not call show-level playback, suppresses wrong-series evidence and preserves the explicit Street Hawk comparison. A fresh-playback browser check, with no prior active player, observed stronger snow, an audio-source start and clearing. Remote endpoints and the published catalog were checked. These observations do not establish audible hardware output or physical-phone behavior. ADB was disconnected during this check.
