## Retro television and commercial inspiration

[MyRetroTVs](https://www.myretrotvs.com/) and its decade television experiences, original interfaces, television artwork and catalog are created by [Joey Cato](https://www.joeycato.com/).

[Nikolay1243’s retro-tv-webos](https://github.com/Nikolay1243/retro-tv-webos) is an independent webOS launcher for those original sites. Its [attribution document](https://github.com/Nikolay1243/retro-tv-webos/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION.md) identifies Joey Cato’s work and the original video providers. It inspired this YATA feature request.

YATA independently implements optional between-story commercial breaks using its existing bumper scheduler. Its starter pack uses individually reviewed Internet Archive video sources, credited in data/commercials-discovery.json. Source dates supplied in titles are recorded separately from modern upload dates.

The MyRetroTVs source investigation inspected public delivered scripts and catalog data to identify its YouTube-backed commercial category. YATA’s shipped code, artwork and commercial starter catalog are independently assembled. Credit is not a grant of rights to any referenced artwork, catalog or media, and does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

## Library metadata and artwork

`data/library-art.json` adapts metadata from [TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/api), with reviewed series matches, premiere-year corrections and source links. TVmaze data is provided under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); the adapted metadata is provided under the same license. Supplemental facts and item images link to their Wikipedia or Internet Archive records. Each entry retains its metadata page and original image URL.

Show artwork, title logos, posters and archive thumbnails retain their respective owners’ rights. Images are resized to WebP and displayed as subdued decorative backgrounds. No generated show art is used. The data license is not a blanket license for depicted show artwork.
